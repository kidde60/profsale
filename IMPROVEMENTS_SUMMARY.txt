🎨 DESIGN SYSTEM
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

COLOR PALETTE
  • Primary:    Amber (#FBBF24)      - Buttons, links, accents
  • Dark:       Slate-950 (#030712)  - Backgrounds
  • Light:      White (#FFFFFF)      - Cards, panels
  • Success:    Emerald (#10B981)    - Positive states
  • Warning:    Amber (#F59E0B)      - Alerts
  • Danger:     Rose (#F43F5E)       - Errors
  • Neutral:    Slate-500 (#64748B)  - Secondary text

TYPOGRAPHY
  • H1: 30px, Semibold, Tracking-tight
  • H2: 24px, Semibold, Tracking-tight
  • H3: 18px, Semibold
  • Body: 14-16px, Regular
  • Small: 12px, Regular

SPACING
  • Base Unit: 4px
  • Scale: 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 40, 48px

BORDER RADIUS
  • Small:  8px (rounded-lg)
  • Medium: 12px (rounded-xl)
  • Large:  16px (rounded-2xl)
  • XL:     24px (rounded-3xl)
